Land & Title Clearance
Do this first — everything else depends on it
STEP 1.1

7/12 Satbara Extract

Confirms ownership, area, encumbrances, survey number. Your name (Mr. Dyaneshwar Satbhai) must appear clearly as the owner. Check for any liens, mortgages, or encumbrances.

📍 Talathi office or online: bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in
⏱ 1–3 days | Cost: Nominal
STEP 1.2

Property Card (City Survey Record)

Confirms plot measurements match your approved plan. Verifies SR No. 56, Plot 7B dimensions. Essential for AutoDCR submission later.

📍 City Survey Office, Nashik Road
⏱ 1–7 days | Cost: Nominal
STEP 1.3

30-Year Title Search Report

Engage a local advocate to search all registrations on this plot for the past 30 years. Confirms no dispute, court case, or mortgage. Banks require this for buyer home loans. Your buyers will not get loans without a clean title.

📍 Sub-Registrar office records via local advocate
⏱ 7–15 days | Cost: ₹5,000–15,000
STEP 1.4

NA Order (Non-Agricultural Permission) — If Required

If 7/12 shows Class-A/B agricultural land, you must convert it to NA before any building permission. This is your single longest-lead-time item. Apply immediately if needed — do not wait.

📍 Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO), Nashik
⏱ 2–4 months | Cost: ₹50–200/sqm premium to government
STEP 1.5

Index II Copy

Certified copy of all past property registrations. Confirms chain of ownership is clean.

📍 Sub-Registrar office, Nashik Road
⏱ 1–3 days | Cost: ₹200–500
Stage 2 — Technical & Architectural Preparation Indraprastha Heights, Nashik
2
Technical & Architectural Documents
Guru Buildcon is your planner — confirm all items below with them
STEP 2.1

Confirm Architect's CoA Registration

Your architect (Guru Buildcon) must be registered with the Council of Architecture (CoA). Their CoA number goes on every drawing submission. Verify this — NMC will reject plans from unlicensed architects.

📍 Verify at: coa.gov.in
STEP 2.2

Appoint Licensed Structural Engineer

Separate from the architect. Must hold a valid structural engineering license recognized under UDCPR 2020 in Maharashtra. Signs all structural drawings and provides a stability certificate at each construction stage.

⏱ Appoint now — needed for soil test too
STEP 2.3

Soil Test / Bore Log Report

Mandatory geotechnical investigation of your plot. Determines foundation type (isolated, combined, raft or pile). Structural engineer cannot finalize design without this. Hire a geotechnical lab in Nashik.

⏱ 7–14 days | Cost: ₹15,000–40,000
STEP 2.4

Prepare AutoDCR-Compatible Drawing Set

NMC uses AutoDCR for online building plan submission. All drawings must be in DXF/DWG format with correct layer naming. Required drawings:

  • Site plan (plot, roads, setbacks clearly marked)
  • Floor plans — all floors including terrace & stilt/ground parking
  • Elevations — all 4 sides (North, South, East, West)
  • Sections — minimum 2 (one longitudinal, one cross)
  • Parking layout plan (mandatory under UDCPR)
  • Drainage & sewage disposal plan
  • Rainwater harvesting plan (mandatory for NMC)
  • Solar water heater provision plan (mandatory for new buildings)

Stage 3 — NOC Collection (Run All in Parallel) Indraprastha Heights, Nashik
3
NOC Collection
Biggest time consumer — start ALL simultaneously, not one by one
Most Common Delay Cause: Builders apply for NOCs sequentially. Apply for ALL listed NOCs on Day 1. NMC will not sanction your building plan without every NOC in the list below.
NOC 3.1 — MANDATORY

Fire Department NOC

Mandatory for G+4 and above buildings. Submit fire exit plan, wet riser design, fire extinguisher layout, fire staircase dimensions, smoke detection plan to NMC Fire Department.

📍 Director of Fire Services, Nashik Municipal Corporation
⏱ 30–60 days | Cost: Nominal govt fee + fire drawing preparation
NOC 3.2 — MANDATORY

AAI Height Clearance (Airport Authority of India)

Nashik has Ozar Airport. Your building must be checked against airport funnel height restrictions. Apply online — provide GPS coordinates of your plot and proposed building height. Do not skip this — NMC rejects plans without it.

📍 Online: nocas.aai.aero
⏱ 15–45 days | Cost: ₹500–2,000 application fee
NOC 3.3 — MANDATORY

MSEDCL — Electrical Service Connection NOC

Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd. NOC for power connection to the building. Required before building permission is sanctioned.

📍 MSEDCL Nashik Division office
⏱ 15–30 days
NOC 3.4 — MANDATORY

NMC Water & Drainage Connection NOC

Water supply connection NOC + drainage connection to NMC municipal sewer. Show drainage disposal plan in your drawings. Both must be submitted to NMC's water department.

📍 NMC Water Department, Nashik Road Ward Office
⏱ 15–30 days
NOC 3.5 — IF APPLICABLE

Tree Authority NOC

If any existing trees on the plot need to be removed for construction. Heavy penalties for unauthorized tree removal in NMC limits. If no trees on plot, obtain a self-declaration certificate instead.

📍 NMC Tree Authority
⏱ 30–60 days if applicable
NOC 3.6 — CHECK IF APPLICABLE

Cantonment / Defense NOC

Nashik Road has a cantonment zone nearby. If your plot falls within cantonment jurisdiction or the restricted zone, an additional NOC from Station Commander, Deolali/Nashik Road Cantonment is required. Verify with your advocate and architect immediately.

📍 Station HQ, Nashik Road Cantonment
NOC 3.7 — MANDATORY for this project size

MPCB — Maharashtra Pollution Control Board

Environmental clearance for construction projects above a threshold area. Apply online. Required for NMC building sanction.

📍 Online: environs.maharashtra.gov.in
⏱ 30–60 days
Pro Tip: Create a NOC tracker sheet with application date, reference number, expected date, and status for each NOC. Chase each office every 10 days in person — physical follow-up works significantly faster than phone or email in Nashik.

NMC AutoDCR portal — architect submits on your behalf

Your architect submits DXF/DWG drawings through NMC's online building permission portal. AutoDCR system auto-checks compliance with UDCPR 2020. All NOCs collected in Stage 3 must be attached as scanned documents.

STEP 4.3

NMC Technical Scrutiny

NMC's Town Planning department reviews all drawings. They may raise written objections (queries). Your architect must respond to each query in writing with revised drawings or justification. Common objections: FSI calculation error, parking shortfall, setback violations, fire staircase width insufficient.

⏱ 30–90 days
STEP 4.4

Building Plan Sanction Letter Received

NMC issues the formal building plan sanction (previously called IOD — Intimation of Disapproval under old regulations). This approves your plan subject to conditions. Keep multiple certified copies — needed for RERA, bank, buyers.

5
MahaRERA Registration
Mandatory before any marketing, advertising, booking, or accepting money
Legal Requirement: Accepting even ₹1 as booking amount without RERA registration is a criminal offence under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. Do not market or accept bookings before this step.
STEP 5.1

Register on MahaRERA Portal

📍 maharera.mahaonline.gov.in

Documents required: building plan sanction, land ownership documents, architect's certificate, CA certificate (project cost & financials), promoter PAN, Aadhaar, bank details.

STEP 5.2

Open Dedicated Project Escrow Account

Under RERA, 70% of all money collected from buyers must go into a separate designated bank account and can only be used for that project's construction. Open this account before RERA registration — account details are submitted as part of RERA application.

STEP 5.3

Declare Project Details & Get RERA Number

Declare all unit details (carpet areas only — RERA prohibits super built-up area sales), prices, amenities, completion date. RERA number is issued within 30 days of complete application. Every advertisement, hoarding, WhatsApp message, and brochure must display this RERA number.

⏱ 30 days | Cost: Registration fee based on project area
STEP 5.4

Quarterly Progress Updates

Once registered, you must upload construction progress photographs and financial updates to the MahaRERA portal every quarter throughout construction. Non-compliance attracts penalties.

Commencement Certificate (CC)
Legal permission to break ground — do not start construction before this
STEP 6.1

Apply for Commencement Certificate

Apply to NMC Building Department after receiving building plan sanction (Stage 4) and RERA registration (Stage 5). Submit both documents with your CC application.

STEP 6.2

NMC Site Inspection

NMC engineer physically visits the plot to confirm boundaries match the approved plan, no illegal construction has started, and setbacks are physically marked on site.

STEP 6.3

CC Issued — Construction Can Begin

Once CC is in hand, you can legally begin foundation work. Post the CC and approved plan on-site (NMC requirement — must be visible at all times during construction).

8
Completion & Occupancy Certificate (OC)
Without OC, flats cannot be legally handed over and buyers cannot get bank loans
STEP 8.1

Completion Certificate Application to NMC

Submit with: architect's completion certificate (building as per sanctioned plan), structural engineer's stability certificate, fire department final NOC, lift inspection certificate, rainwater harvesting completion certificate, solar system installation certificate.

STEP 8.2

NMC Final Physical Inspection

NMC team inspects every floor. Any deviations from the sanctioned plan are flagged. Minor deviations may be compounded (penalty payment to regularize). Major deviations require demolition. Build exactly as per approved plan.

STEP 8.3

Occupancy Certificate (OC) Issued

The most important document. Without OC: flats cannot be legally handed over, buyers cannot get home loans from banks, society cannot be formed, water/electricity connections may be regularized. Handing over possession before OC is an offence under RERA.

⏱ 2–3 months from final inspection application
Stage 9 — Post Construction  |  Timeline  |  Key Contacts Indraprastha Heights, Nashik
9
Post-Construction — Handover Stage
After OC is received
STEP 9.1

Form Co-operative Housing Society (CHS)

Mandatory under Maharashtra law. Must be formed within 4 months of majority of flats being sold. Register with the District Deputy Registrar, Co-operative Societies, Nashik. Governed by Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960. Draft bye-laws and elect committee at formation meeting.

STEP 9.2

Conveyance of Land to Society

Transfer land ownership to the registered society via conveyance deed. Mandatory under MOFA (Maharashtra Ownership Flats Act, 1963). If builder delays, society can apply for deemed conveyance through District Deputy Registrar. Complete this promptly — buyers will demand it.

STEP 9.3

Individual MSEDCL Meter Connections

Individual electricity meters for each flat. Separate common area meter for lift, passage lighting, water pump, terrace lights. Apply to MSEDCL Nashik after OC is in hand.

STEP 9.4

Property Tax Registration with NMC

Register each flat individually with NMC for property tax assessment. Buyers need this document for home loan processing and ownership proof. Issue tax receipts to buyers from Day 1 of possession.
