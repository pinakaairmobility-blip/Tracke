The brief

A brand film for MeghQuasar, the broker-only air charter desk working the Gulf, India, Africa and Europe. मेघ (megh) is Sanskrit for cloud; a quasar is among the most luminous objects in the universe. The film follows the name: light cutting through cloud, then the desk's work made visible, from sourcing and feasibility to compliance and like-for-like quotes.

How it's built

One HTML file. Six three.js scenes with custom GLSL, a bloom and god-ray post stack, a kinetic type layer, and a score synthesized in Web Audio. Every frame is a pure function of time, so the same file plays live here and renders frame-accurately to MP4.

Distances are great-circle values computed from airport coordinates. Aircraft range and quote bars are illustrative, and marked on screen.
